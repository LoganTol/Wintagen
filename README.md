# Wintagen Landing Page

Lovable Build Prompt — Wintagen Landing Page

Create a polished, responsive landing page for Wintagen, a software umbrella company that both builds its own digital products and provides professional technology services.

This is a front-end marketing website only. Do not add authentication, a database, payments, a CMS, or other backend functionality. Build the page so its copy, cards, and links are easy to edit later.

Primary goal

Make Wintagen feel like a credible, established parent brand that can support a growing portfolio of products and service lines. A visitor should understand within a few seconds that Wintagen does two things:

Creates focused software products.

Provides practical technology services to clients.

The experience should be clean, light, calm, and easy to navigate. Avoid the visual clichés of an AI startup.

Brand and visual direction

Company name: Wintagen

Primary brand color: #058484

Use #058484 for the logo, wordmark, primary buttons, links, selected states, and restrained accents.

Use a warm white or very light neutral page background.

Use deep charcoal or blue-black for primary text and accessible gray for secondary text.

Alternate selected sections with a very subtle cool-gray or pale-teal background.

Use a modern sans-serif typeface with crisp, readable typography.

Create generous whitespace, a consistent grid, and a smooth vertical rhythm.

Use restrained rounded corners, thin borders, and very soft shadows.

Add only subtle hover and scroll-reveal motion, and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Do not use loud gradients, neon colors, excessive glass effects, stock photos of office workers, fake dashboards, carousels, autoplay media, or heavy parallax.

Keep the design original and professional; do not imitate a specific existing website.

If no logo asset is provided, create a simple abstract geometric logo placeholder next to the Wintagen wordmark. It should feel precise and modular, suggest multiple efforts connected under one company, and use #058484. Do not create a complicated illustrated logo.

Header

Create a light, spacious header.

On desktop, display:

Wintagen logo and wordmark on the left

Products

Services

About Us

A right-aligned Contact button

Products, Services, and About Us must all appear across the top of the desktop header. The wordmark should return the visitor to the top of the page. Each navigation item should smoothly scroll to its matching homepage section. The header can gain a subtle translucent background, bottom border, or small shadow once the user scrolls.

On mobile, use an accessible menu with a clear open and close control, comfortable touch targets, keyboard support, and visible focus states.

Page structure and exact starter copy

1. Hero

Use a balanced two-column desktop layout that stacks naturally on mobile.

Eyebrow:
Products. Services. One standard.

H1:
We build software—and the company behind it.

Supporting copy:
Wintagen creates focused digital products and provides technology services for businesses ready to build, improve, or move an idea forward.

Primary button:
Explore our products

Secondary button:
View our services

On the right, create a refined abstract composition using modular cards, connected shapes, or subtle interface-like panels to represent a portfolio of products and services. Do not display invented analytics or fake customer data.

2. Company model

Section heading:
Two ways we create value.

Create two large, equal cards:

Products
Focused software designed around clear, real-world needs.
Link: Explore products

Services
Practical technology support for organizations building or improving digital experiences.
Link: Explore services

Give the cards distinct visual cues while keeping them within one coherent design system.

3. Products

Add the section ID products.

Heading:
Products built with purpose.

Supporting copy:
Our portfolio is designed to grow. Each product starts with a specific problem and a straightforward reason to exist.

Because final product information has not been supplied, do not invent product names, features, users, or results. Show one polished editable placeholder card with:

Label: Wintagen Portfolio

Title: Product announcements coming soon.

Description: We're preparing the first products in the Wintagen portfolio. More details will be shared here as they become available.

Status badge: In development

Build this as a reusable card component so more product cards can be added later without changing the layout.

4. Services

Add the section ID services.

Heading:
Technology services, shaped around the work.

Create a responsive grid of four editable service cards:

Software development
Purpose-built web applications and digital tools.

Web experiences
Modern, responsive websites designed for clarity and conversion.

Product strategy
Practical guidance to define, validate, and scope digital products.

Automation and integrations
Connected workflows that reduce repetitive work.

Add a button below the grid:
Discuss a project

Treat these service descriptions as easy-to-edit initial content, not permanent claims.

5. About Us

Add the section ID about.

Heading:
Built to create useful things.

Body copy:
Wintagen is a software company built around a simple idea: good technology should have a clear purpose. We develop our own products and apply the same practical thinking to client work—combining thoughtful design, dependable execution, and room to grow.

Show three compact principles:

Clarity before complexity

Useful over impressive

Built for the next stage

Do not invent a founding date, headquarters, team size, founder biography, client list, or company timeline.

6. Final call to action

Add the section ID contact.

Heading:
Have a product to explore or a project to discuss?

Supporting copy:
See what we're building or start a conversation about what you need.

Primary button:
Explore products

Secondary button:
Contact Wintagen

Until a real contact destination is provided, make the contact action easy to configure and do not create a fake form submission flow.

7. Footer

Include:

Wintagen logo and wordmark

Descriptor: Software products and technology services.

Links: Products, Services, About Us, Contact, Privacy, Terms

Dynamic copyright year

Do not add social icons unless real social profiles are provided.

UX and responsive behavior

Use a clear visual hierarchy and keep paragraphs comfortably narrow.

Make the primary journey obvious: understand Wintagen, choose Products or Services, then take the relevant action.

Ensure cards align cleanly and reflow elegantly on tablets and phones.

Preserve logical reading order at all screen sizes.

Use one H1 and correctly ordered section headings.

Ensure every button and link has a descriptive label.

Add keyboard-accessible interactions, visible focus states, and WCAG AA contrast.

Avoid teal text on white when the text size would not meet contrast requirements; use the brand color primarily for controls and larger accents.

Respect the user's reduced-motion setting.

SEO metadata

Page title:
Wintagen | Software Products & Technology Services

Meta description:
Wintagen creates focused software products and provides practical technology services for businesses building what comes next.

Final quality check

Before considering the page complete, verify that:

The desktop header visibly includes Products, Services, and About Us.

All navigation and CTA links reach the correct sections.

Wintagen and its logo use #058484.

Products and Services feel distinct but equally important.

No unsupported products, testimonials, clients, metrics, awards, or company facts were invented.

The design is polished on mobile, tablet, laptop, and wide desktop.

The page feels light, professional, restrained, and ready to expand.
